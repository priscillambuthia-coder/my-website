Dubai is a city and emirate in the United Arab Emirates known for luxury shopping, ultramodern architecture and a lively nightlife scene. Burj Khalifa, an 830m-tall tower, dominates the skyscraper-filled skyline. At its foot lies Dubai Fountain, with jets and lights choreographed to music. On artificial islands just offshore is Atlantis, The Palm, a resort with water and marine-animal parks Dubai[a] is the most populous city in the United Arab Emirates and the capital of the Emirate of Dubai. It is on a creek on the southeastern coast of the Persian Gulf. As of 2025, its population stands at 4 million, 92% of whom are expatriates. The wider urban area includes Sharjah and has a population of 5 million people as of 2023, while the Dubai-Sharjah-Ajman metropolitan area has a population of 6 million people. Founded in the early 18th century as a pearling and fishing settlement, Dubai became a regional trade hub in the 20th century after declaring itself a free port (1901) and extending the Creek (1961) Modest oil revenue helped accelerate Dubai's development from the 1960s to the 1990s, when the city started to diversify its economy. In 2018, oil production contributed less than 1% to the emirate's GDP.

My Favourite Dubai Experiences

Ferrari World

Ferrari World Abu Dhabi (Arabic: عالم فيراري أبوظبي) is a mostly indoors theme park located on Yas Island in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. It is the world's first Ferrari-themed park and is the home of Formula Rossa, which held the record for the world’s fastest roller coaster from 2010 till the opening of Falcons Flight

House of Hype

House of Hype, located in Dubai Mall (Level FV-L1), is a massive 100,000-sq-ft immersive entertainment destination featuring over 100+ experiences, including digital art, interactive gaming, neon-themed zones, and live performances. Designed for all ages, it blends technology with entertainment, including K-Pop stages and, 25+ surreal zones, and offers tickets starting around AED 119-149. Key Features and Attractions Immersive Zones: Features, neon Tokyo streets, glowing gardens, and futuristic,, surreal environments. Interactive Gaming: Over 50+ stations, including full-body, adventures, and arcades, allowing visitors to accumulate digital coins. Photo Ops & Technology: Includes, mirror rooms, robot light shows, and, digital fashion, stations, ideal for content creation. Hidden Elements: Secret, rooms, such as a, concealed, sushi bar and, secret, portals, are hidden within the, attraction. Shopping & Dining: Offers, exclusive,, limited-edition, streetwear, gear, and, treats.

Dubai Mall

Dubai Mall is the largest shopping mall in Dubai. The mall is part of Downtown Dubai and is located adjacent to the Burj Khalifa. It includes over 1,200 shops

Museum of the future

 Museum of the Future (Arabic: متحف المستقبل)[1] is a building located in the Financial District of Dubai, UAE. The museum, with 7 floors, is dedicated to exploring the future of science, technology, and innovation. It is housed in a torus-shaped building with windows in the form of a poem in Arabic about the future, written by Dubai ruler Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum

Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization

As directed by His Highness Sheikh Dr. Sultan bin Mohammed AlQasimi, Supreme Council Member and Ruler of Sharjah, the Sharjah Museums Authority ــ SMA was established in 2006 as an autonomous government authority. SMA links and oversees 16 museums across Sharjah that cover widely varied fields, including Islamic art and culture, archaeology, heritage, science, marine life, as well as the history of Sharjah and the region. Our vision is to be a cultural beacon that enhances Sharjah’s identity locally and internationally and contribute in nurturing a community aware of museums’ importance as a cultural, educational and enjoyable destination. Our mission is to deliver the highest museum standards to preserve collections & enhance an appreciation of culture and learning through our exhibitions, educational and community programs.

City Tour

A Dubai city tour offers a mix of modern marvels and historic charm, typically covering sights like the Burj Khalifa, Burj Al Arab, Dubai Mall, Palm Jumeirah, and old Dubai's Al Fahidi area with its souks (Gold, Spice) and traditional abra ride on Dubai Creek, with options to see the Museum of the Future or Jumeirah Mosque. Tours range from half-day introductions to multi-day packages, often including hotel transfers, expert guides, and photo stops at key landmarks.

Desert Safari Dinner

A Dubai desert safari dinner typically features a comprehensive evening in a Bedouin-style camp, offering a buffet with BBQ (veg/non-veg), unlimited refreshments, and cultural entertainment like belly dancing and Tanoura shows. Activities often include dune bashing, camel rides, sandboarding, and henna painting. Prices usually range from 115-125 AED.
